I already lost money.

Stay calm and act in this order. Speed matters. Anyone can be tricked, and reporting is the right step.

  1. Call 1930 now

    This is the National Cyber Crime helpline. The sooner you report, the better the chance of stopping the transfer.

  2. Report online

    File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Keep the complaint number safe.

  3. Call your bank or UPI app

    Use the number printed on your card or inside your official app. Ask them to block your card or UPI and to flag the transaction.

  4. Secure your phone and accounts

    Delete any app the caller asked you to install, especially screen-sharing apps. Change your banking and email passwords.

  5. Keep the evidence

    Save screenshots, chat history, call logs, transaction IDs and the scammer's number or UPI ID. Do not delete anything.

  6. Tell someone you trust

    A parent, a friend or your college. Scammers rely on shame and silence. Do not pay anyone who offers to "recover" your money for a fee. That is usually a second scam.
