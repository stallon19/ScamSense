About ScamSense.

A calm, plain-English tool that helps students and families in India spot scams and say no.

Why it exists

Fake internship offers, fake scholarships, UPI refund tricks and fake KYC calls cost people real money every day. Most tools only tell you "scam" or "not scam". That does not help the next time. ScamSense shows you the proof and then lets you practise, so you can spot the trick yourself.

How the AI works

  • You send a message or a screenshot. ScamSense passes it to Google Gemini with strict instructions to analyse it and never follow anything written inside it.
  • Gemini returns a verdict, the red flags and the exact words that look suspicious. ScamSense checks that those words really appear in your message before it underlines them.
  • In Practice Mode, a second set of instructions makes Gemini play a scammer. At the end, another request scores your replies and quotes your own words back to you.

What it cannot do

  • It can be wrong. Do not use a result as proof for a bank, the police or a court.
  • It does not open links, call numbers or check if a website or phone number is registered.
  • It is an assistant. It does not act on its own or contact anyone for you.

Privacy in short

ScamSense does not store your messages and has no accounts. Your text is sent to Google Gemini to be analysed. Read the full details on the privacy and terms page.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. ScamSense runs on the free Gemini tier, so if many people use it at once you may see a message asking you to wait a minute.

Does it save my messages?

ScamSense does not store them. If you use the recent checks list, a short note is saved only in your own browser, and you can clear it any time.

Which languages work?

You can paste English, Hindi or Hinglish messages. Explanations come in English or Hindi.

Is it an AI agent?

No. It is an AI assistant. It answers when you ask and does not take actions on its own.

What if I already lost money?

Call 1930 and visit the help checklist right away.
