Privacy and terms.

Short and honest. Last updated 9 October 2026.

What happens to your message

  • When you press Check message, your text or screenshot is sent from our server to the Google Gemini API to be analysed. In Practice Mode, your chat is sent the same way.
  • ScamSense does not store your messages, screenshots or chats. It does not write them to logs.
  • Identical text checks are kept in server memory for up to 30 minutes so that repeat checks are fast. This is cleared when the server restarts.
  • Google processes what we send. This project uses the free Gemini API tier. Under Google's terms for that tier, Google may use content sent to improve its products. See Google's Gemini API terms for details.
  • Please do not paste passwords, OTPs, PINs, card numbers or other secrets into ScamSense.

What stays on your device

  • Your theme choice.
  • If you open the Check page, a short list of recent checks (verdict and the first words of the message). It is stored only in your browser. You can clear it with "Clear history".

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking scripts and no cookies for analytics.
  • Your network address is held in memory for a few minutes only, to limit how many requests one person can make.

Terms of use

  • ScamSense gives general information only. It is not legal, financial or police advice.
  • Results come from an AI and can be wrong. A "Safe" result is not a guarantee. Always verify through your bank's official app or the number on your card.
  • Do not use ScamSense to harm others. Practice Mode is for learning to resist scams only.
  • The service is provided as is, without warranty.

Contact for fraud

If you are a victim of fraud, call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in. See the help checklist.
