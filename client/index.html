Scams target students every day.

They look different, but they use the same few tricks. Once you can see the trick, it stops working.

Internships that ask you to pay.

A real employer never charges a fee to give you a job, a seat or an internship.

UPI refunds that ask for your PIN.

You never need a PIN to receive money. If someone asks, it is a trap.

KYC messages that threaten to block you.

Banks do not ask for your OTP or card details on a call or an SMS.
